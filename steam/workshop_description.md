# Trouble In Armaville

You don't know who the killers are. Neither does anyone else, except them and each other.

Trouble In Armaville brings Trouble In Terrorist Town to Arma 3: the same hidden-role deduction game that's kept GMod players lying to each other for two decades, now played out with real ballistics, real vehicles, and a sandbox big enough to actually hide a body in. Every round drops a server full of players into a walled-off town with one shared secret: some of you are Traitors, and nobody innocent knows who.

## The pitch

Everyone starts as a civilian with nothing but their eyes and their instincts. A hidden minority are Traitors, working together, arming up, picking people off quietly enough that nobody connects the dots until it's too late. One publicly known Detective stands between the two, armed with actual investigative tools instead of just a hunch. And somewhere in the mix, a Jester who wins by getting an innocent person to make the worst possible call.

Nobody announces a death. A body has to be found, and even then it takes real investigation to learn who they were and what killed them. That gap between "someone's missing" and "here's what actually happened" is where the whole game lives: the accusations, the second-guessing, the Traitor sweating through a conversation they should've avoided.

Bring it to a full Arma server and it stops being a guessing game about hitboxes and starts being one about people: who hesitated, who's not where they said they'd be, who really needed that silenced pistol.

## Why it holds up round after round

A fresh arena every round, on any of five stock terrains, so no two games play out on the same ground twice. Credits earned for playing your role well, spent on gear built to shift the investigation as much as the fight. Karma that remembers how you played last round. And a genuine reason to keep believing the person next to you, right up until you don't.

New to TTT? A first-timer's guide sits right in the map screen, and every round opens with a quiet, private reminder of exactly what your role is and who you're allowed to trust.

## One thing to fix before you host

Turn off **Kill Messages** in your server's difficulty settings first. It's the one thing standing between this being a mystery and Arma just telling everyone who the killer was. Details on the [GitHub page](https://github.com/AdamWaldie/TTTARMA3).
